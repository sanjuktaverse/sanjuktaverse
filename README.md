## ◉ &nbsp;What I Build

### &nbsp;&nbsp;`01` — NLP & Machine Learning

> **Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis** — E-Commerce Reviews
>
> Classified product reviews into positive / negative / neutral at the aspect level.  
> Benchmarked Decision Tree, SVM, Naive Bayes and others — Decision Tree won at **98% accuracy**.  
> Delivered during industrial internship at Ardent Computech × FIEM.
>
> `Python` · `NLTK` · `scikit-learn` · `Pandas` · `Matplotlib` · `Feature Engineering`
>
> [![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_on_GitHub-1a1a2e?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=d4a843)](https://github.com)

---

### &nbsp;&nbsp;`02` — Embedded Systems & IoT

> **Automatic Fall Detection & Alert System** *(Live Project)*
>
> Real-time health monitor for elderly users built on **ESP32**.  
> Sensors: MPU6050 (fall), MAX30102 (heart rate + SpO₂), NEO-6M (GPS location).  
> Fires emergency alerts automatically. Remote dashboard via **Blynk IoT**.
>
> `C++` · `ESP32` · `MPU6050` · `MAX30102` · `Blynk` · `GPS (NEO-6M)`
>
> [![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_on_GitHub-1a1a2e?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=d4a843)](https://github.com)

---

### &nbsp;&nbsp;`03` — Recommendation Systems

> **Movie Recommendation Engine**
>
> Content-based engine using **TF-IDF vectorization** and **cosine similarity** to suggest movies matching user-selected features. Returns ranked top-N recommendations.
>
> `Python` · `TF-IDF` · `Cosine Similarity` · `scikit-learn` · `Pandas`
>
> [![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_on_GitHub-1a1a2e?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=d4a843)](https://github.com)
